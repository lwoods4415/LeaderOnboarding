# Pricing (approved 2026-10-19)

Program: **Leading People** (first-time leaders, or leaders who never had onboarding). Later programs
(Leading Leaders, Leading a Function…) are priced separately, with a ~20% "leadership pathway" bundle discount.

## Individuals

| Tier | Price | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free preview | $0 (email sign-up) | Week 0 module, self-assessment, one AI practice conversation with scored feedback |
| Essentials | $49 one-time | Full 12-week program, all scenarios, toolkit, growth tracking, one AI practice conversation per module (7) |
| Complete | $149 one-time; **$99 launch price for the first 100 buyers** | Everything, plus unlimited AI practice (within daily limits), "Create your own" scenarios, program tailored to their role and industry |
| Cohort | $399 one-time | Complete, plus 6 live 1-hour group Leader Labs with Lauren, every two weeks |
| 1:1 coaching | ~$150/session (optional, limited) | |

- Essentials buyers can upgrade to Complete for the price difference (offered in-app once their practice runs out).
- 30-day money-back guarantee.
- "Ask your employer to reimburse" request template.
- Access: 12 months.

- Company programs are tailored from an intake form and go live **after Lauren approves them**.
- Optional facilitated Leader Labs: $1,500–2,500 per cohort (7 sessions, up to 10 leaders).
- First 3–5 companies: 30–50% off in exchange for a case study and testimonial.

## Go-to-market notes

- Funnel: organic short video (LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok) and small paid tests ($20–30/day) → free preview → Essentials/Complete → Cohort.
- Companies are the volume lever. Individuals are a route in ("bring this to your team" referral reward).
- Use pilot testimonials (with permission).
